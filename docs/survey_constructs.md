# Survey Constructs and Outcomes

## Employee-Experience Dimensions

The survey will measure six primary employee-experience dimensions.

### Engagement

The employee's motivation, enthusiasm, sense of accomplishment, and willingness to recommend the organization.

### Manager Effectiveness

The extent to which the employee experiences clear expectations, useful feedback, fairness, communication, and support from their immediate manager.

### Psychological Safety

The employee's comfort with raising concerns, asking for help, acknowledging mistakes, and sharing different perspectives.

### Growth and Development

The employee's access to skill development, career opportunities, professional support, and a potential future within the organization.

### Workload Sustainability

The extent to which workload, staffing, time demands, and work pace are manageable and sustainable.

### Belonging

The employee's sense of inclusion, respect, acceptance, and connection to the organization and colleagues.

## Primary Outcome

The primary outcome will be **intent to stay**, measured through multiple survey items rather than a single question.

Intent to stay will be treated as an employee attitude measured at the time of the survey. Associations between employee-experience dimensions and intent to stay will not be presented as proof that one experience dimension causes employees to remain with the organization.

## Future Secondary Outcome

A later version of the project may connect survey responses to voluntary turnover during a defined period following the survey.

The turnover model will be secondary to the employee-listening analysis. It will be evaluated using:

* Time-based validation
* Calibration
* Precision and recall
* Subgroup performance
* Clearly stated limitations

Individual turnover-risk scores will not appear in the public dashboard.

## Survey Construct and Item Counts

For the first iteration of this project, the survey will use four items for each employee-experience construct.

| Construct or measure        | Number of items |
| --------------------------- | --------------: |
| Engagement                  |               4 |
| Manager effectiveness       |               4 |
| Psychological safety        |               4 |
| Growth and development      |               4 |
| Workload sustainability     |               4 |
| Belonging                   |               4 |
| Intent to stay              |               2 |
| Overall employee experience |     1, optional |
| Open-ended comment          |               1 |

The survey contains 26 core Likert-scale items: 24 employee-experience items and two intent-to-stay items.

The optional overall employee-experience item would increase the total to 27 Likert-scale items. The survey will also include one open-ended comment question.

## Response Scale

All Likert-scale items will use a five-point agreement scale:

1. Strongly disagree
2. Disagree
3. Neither agree nor disagree
4. Agree
5. Strongly agree

## Scale Construction

Items intended to measure the same employee-experience dimension will be evaluated before scale scores are constructed.

The evaluation will include:

* Missing-data assessment
* Item distributions
* Item-total relationships
* Reliability analysis
* Exploratory factor analysis

Scale scores will only be created after the item-level results provide sufficient support for combining the relevant items.
