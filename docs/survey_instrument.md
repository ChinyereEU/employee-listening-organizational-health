# Employee Listening Survey Instrument

## Instrument Overview

This document contains the employee-listening survey instrument developed for the Employee Listening and Organizational Health Analytics portfolio project.

The survey measures six employee-experience dimensions:

- Engagement
- Manager effectiveness
- Psychological safety
- Growth and development
- Workload sustainability
- Belonging

The survey also includes two intent-to-stay items, one optional overall employee-experience item, and one open-ended comment question.

Meridian Workforce Solutions is a fictional organization. This instrument was created for a synthetic portfolio project and will not be administered to real employees as part of this analysis.

## Survey Introduction

Thank you for participating in the Meridian Workforce Solutions Employee Listening Survey.

The purpose of this survey is to better understand employees' experiences at work and identify areas where the organization may need additional attention or support.

Please answer each question based on your current experience. There are no right or wrong answers.

## Confidentiality and Reporting

Survey results will be analyzed and reported at the group level.

Results will only be displayed for organizational groups with at least 10 usable survey responses. Individual responses, employee identifiers, and individual retention-risk scores will not be included in public reports or dashboards.

## Item and Scale Notes

- The instrument contains 24 employee-experience items.
- Intent to stay is measured using two additional items.
- The optional overall employee-experience item is not automatically included in a multi-item scale.
- The open-ended comment question uses an unrestricted text response.
- None of the closed-ended items are currently reverse-coded.
- All item wording and variable names should remain consistent with the construct-to-item map and data dictionary.
- Scale scores will only be constructed after item distributions, item-total relationships, reliability, and factor structure have been evaluated.
